What's new in 1.1.7:

• Path: a new tab to progress level by level. Each level is a collection of courses, ending with a quiz.
• Daily course: one course a day to keep your streak going, with a reminder at the time you choose.
• App blocker: choose the app that distracts you and it stays locked until you've finished your daily course.
• Audio mode: listen to your courses like a podcast, from the Lock Screen and offline, in French and English.
• Question of the day: one question every day, in Featured, as a notification and in a new widget for your Home Screen and Lock Screen.
• Over 100 new courses, including courses written by PhDs and professors, and 9 new collections.

Bug fixes and improvements.
