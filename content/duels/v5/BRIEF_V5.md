# Batch v5 — additional instructions (on top of content/duels/v4/v4_BRIEF_WRITER.md)

The app already has ~1 720 questions. This batch adds 1 500 more. The owner's priorities:
**quality, no repetition, many different topics.**

- Stay inside YOUR LANE (sub-topics listed in your task). Another writer covers the rest of the
  subject; overlapping lanes create duplicates.
- **Breadth over depth**: never more than 2 questions about the same person, work, event, place or
  myth (counting the existing questions!). Spread over as many distinct topics as possible.
- **Vary the question forms**: not 30 × "Who wrote …?". Mix: who / what / where / when / which came
  first / which is larger / what is the name of / true-false traps / numeric sliders / "which of these
  is NOT…" (sparingly, only when unambiguous) / cause & consequence / definition of a concept.
- Prefer facts that are interesting to learn (a quiz player should think "oh, nice") over trivia that
  only tests memory of a number.
- **Translatability is mandatory**: the question will be rewritten natively in 29 languages. No
  question whose answer depends on an English word, letter, spelling, pun, rhyme, English etymology,
  or on an English title that has no equivalent. A question that cannot make sense in, e.g.,
  Japanese, Arabic or Finnish will be DELETED.
- Avoid giving the gender of the answer away when distractors mix men and women.
- No image questions here (text only).
