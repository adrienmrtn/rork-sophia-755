# Review — science (v4)

Draft: 74 questions. Final: 67 questions. I fact-checked every draft question and compared each one with existing_science.txt and with the rest of the draft.

## Fixed (5)
- L4 "Who developed the first vaccine, against smallpox, in 1796?" (Jenner): changed to "Which English doctor developed a smallpox vaccine from cowpox in 1796?". The "first" claim is open to dispute: Benjamin Jesty gave cowpox inoculations in 1774, and variolation came even earlier.
- L5 "Which Italian scientist built the first electric battery, in 1800?" (Volta): changed to "…invented the electric battery, in 1800?". I removed "first" to keep the question clear of priority claims such as the "Baghdad battery".
- L6 "Which Arab scholar wrote the influential Book of Optics…" (Ibn al-Haytham): changed to "Which medieval scholar…". "Arab" gave the answer away, because three distractors are Persian. It also avoids an argument about his ethnicity.
- L6 "Which French chemist named oxygen and hydrogen and stated the conservation of mass?" (Lavoisier): changed to "Which chemist gave the elements oxygen and hydrogen their names?". "French" gave the answer away, because all the distractors are British. Credit for conservation of mass is disputed with Lomonosov, so I dropped that part.
- L7 to L8 "Which Indian mathematician set out rules for calculating with zero, in 628 CE?" (Brahmagupta): moved up a level. It fits expert level, and the move fills level 8 after the Kitasato drop.

## Dropped (7)
- L8 "Which Japanese bacteriologist co-discovered the plague bacterium…" (Kitasato): credit is disputed with Yersin. Kitasato's identification is contested, and Yersin is usually named as the discoverer.
- L5 slider "African elephant pregnancy, 22 months": sources give 18 to 22 months, roughly 640 to 660 days, so 21 and 22 are both defensible.
- L5 "Which part of the eye controls how much light enters it?" (Iris): too easy for level 5. Dropped to balance the levels.
- L4 "Which has more bones? newborn / adult": too close to the existing "How many bones in the adult human body? 206".
- L4 tf "heart lies mostly on the right side": the wording is borderline because the heart sits near the midline. Dropped to balance the levels.
- L3 "What is the dense centre of an atom called?": overlaps with the existing atom and particle questions. Dropped to balance the levels.
- L3 tf "Water expands when it freezes": overlaps in topic with the L7 slider on water's maximum density at 4 °C. Dropped to balance the levels.

## Kept unchanged
The other 60 questions are correct, unambiguous, have certainly wrong distractors and are not duplicates. Checks on the sliders:
- Each value is an integer, falls strictly inside its range and is not at the exact midpoint.
- Rounding gives one clear answer: Earth's axial tilt is 23.44° (23), the speed of light is 299,792 km/s (300 thousand), water is densest at 3.98 °C (4), and there are 25 primes below 100.
- Sputnik 1957, Hubble 1990 and Chadwick 1932 are standard dates.
