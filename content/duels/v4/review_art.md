# Review — art (v4)

Draft: 72 questions. Final: 66 (levels 1–5 and 8: 8 each; levels 6–7: 9 each).
Every question was fact-checked (answer, distractors, dates, wording, duplicates against
existing_art.txt, including the image questions). 65 kept as written, 1 fixed, 6 dropped.
No replacements were needed.

## Fixed
- L8 "Which Danish designer created the "Egg" chair in 1958?" — the distractors Alvar Aalto
  (Finnish) and Eero Saarinen (Finnish-American) were not Danish, so the word "Danish" ruled them
  out. Replaced them with Danish designers: Hans Wegner, Verner Panton, Finn Juhl.

## Dropped
- L4 tf "Edvard Munch made several versions of "The Scream"." — the same painting is already
  covered in existing questions (text "Which Norwegian artist painted The Scream?" and the
  image "What is this painting called?"), so this counts as a duplicate.
- L2 mcq "Which Pop artist made famous … portraits of Marilyn Monroe?" (Warhol) — near-duplicate
  of the existing tf "Andy Warhol was a leading figure of Pop art". The word "Pop" in the question
  points to the answer.
- L1 mcq "In which country is the Taj Mahal?" — dropped to reduce the surplus. The Taj Mahal
  would otherwise appear twice in the draft, plus once in the existing questions (Shah Jahan).
  The L2 tf "built as a royal palace" (false) is kept.
- L3 mcq "Which Italian composer wrote "La traviata" and "Aida"?" — the kept L8 slider
  ("Verdi's opera "Aida" premiere in Cairo") gives this answer away.
- L5 slider "In what year did Beethoven die?" — dropped to reduce the surplus. Beethoven was
  over-represented (Moonlight Sonata, piano-sonata count, existing questions). The range
  1760–1900 also put 1827 close to the middle.
- L8 slider "How many numbered piano sonatas did Beethoven write?" — dropped to reduce the
  surplus (same Beethoven over-representation).
