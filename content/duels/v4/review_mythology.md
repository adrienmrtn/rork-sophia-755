# Review — mythology (v4)

Draft: 72 questions (9 per level). Final: 66 questions. Every draft question was fact-checked and compared against existing_mythology.txt and the rest of the draft.

## Fixed (3)
- L4 "Which Egyptian goddess of love and music is often shown as a cow?": changed distractor Isis to Sekhmet. Isis took on Hathor's cow-horn imagery and is sometimes shown as a cow, so it was arguably correct.
- L4 "…which pure knight, son of Lancelot, achieves the Holy Grail?": changed distractor Percival to Tristan. Percival is the Grail winner in Chrétien and Wolfram, and in Malory he also reaches the Grail.
- L8 to L7 "Garuda is the mount of Vishnu" (tf): level 8 was too high for a widely known fact.

## Dropped (6)
- L1 tf "Noah built an ark…": a second question on Noah's flood next to the 40-days slider. It also cuts down the share of Bible questions.
- L2 "Which prophet was swallowed by a great fish?" (Jonah): dropped to cut the Bible share (10 of 72 in the draft) for variety.
- L3 "Which Roman god is the equivalent of the Greek god Hermes?": near-duplicate of the existing tf "Vulcan is the equivalent of Hermes".
- L4 "In which city did people build a tower…" (Babel): dropped to cut the Bible share.
- L5 "Which son of Jacob was sold into slavery?" (Joseph): dropped to cut the Bible share and to balance the levels.
- L7 slider "How many avatars make up the Dashavatara?": a give-away, because *dasha* means "ten" and the name contains the answer. Other lists count more avatars (for example 22 in the Bhagavata Purana).
